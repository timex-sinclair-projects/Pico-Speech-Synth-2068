# Rev B netlist

Generated by `../../tools/revb_netlist.py`; do not edit the outputs by hand.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `zxvoice-revb.net` | Protel/Altium-format netlist. EasyEDA Pro: **File → Import → Netlist**, into a new PCB. |
| `zxvoice-revb-nets.csv` | The same connections as a flat table (net, ref, pin, pin name, part), for checking against the schematic as you draw it. |
| `zxvoice-revb-bom.csv` | Parts with footprint names and the LCSC numbers confirmed so far. |

## Importing into EasyEDA Pro

The netlist names a footprint for every part in the LCSC/EasyEDA naming style
(`TSSOP-20_L6.5-W4.4-P0.65-LS6.4-BL`, `R0603`, ...). The import dialog
matches those names against the library; where a name is not found it lets
you pick a footprint, and the report can be exported as CSV. Expect to map:

- `EDGE-2X23-P2.54-KEY3` — draw this one: 2 × 23 fingers at 2.54 mm, key slot at position 3, TS1000 numbering (A side = component side). Pad names `1A`…`23A`, `1B`…`23B`.
- `SolderJumper-3_P1.3` and `SolderJumper-2_P1.3` — any three-pad / two-pad solder jumper; pad names `1`, `C`, `3` and `1`, `2`.
- `AUDIO-SMD_PJ-320A` — the jack's pad numbering varies by vendor; the nets use `1` sleeve, `2` tip, `3` ring, `4`/`5` switch (unused).
- `LED0603-RD` — pad `1` cathode, `2` anode. Check against the chosen part.
- Diodes `SOD-123`: pad `1` cathode, `2` anode.
- Tactile switches: pads `1`/`2` are one contact, `3`/`4` the other.

Everything else (RP2040, W25Q32, ABM8, AP2112K, LVC245, LVC1G125, HCT688,
HCT138, MAX98357A, USB-C 16-pin, JST-PH) uses the pin numbering of the LCSC
part's own footprint.

The schematic is best drawn in EasyEDA from the design page rather than
imported: a Protel netlist carries connectivity, not symbols. Once drawn,
export EasyEDA's own Protel netlist and diff it against this file; the CSV
makes that a one-minute check.

## Design decisions encoded here

- Port decode is strict by default (JP1–JP6 = 1 1 1 0 0 0, JP7 on the '688
  side). See the design page for the loose-decode option.
- U5's three spare inputs are grounded and their outputs left open.
- The '138 outputs Y0, Y1, Y4, Y6 are open: port 0x07 in both directions,
  IN 0x17 and OUT 0x27 are not used.
- MAX98357A GAIN_SLOT is open (9 dB). SD_MODE is driven by GP19 through 1 kΩ:
  high selects the left channel and enables the amplifier, low shuts it down.
- The line-out second stage is 3.3 kΩ + 30 kΩ with JP8 bridged across the
  30 kΩ: bridged (default) gives the 2.2 kHz corner, open the original 33 kΩ.
- Power: bus 5 V → F1 (500 mA PTC) → D1; USB VBUS → D2; both to +5V.
  +3V3 from U3 feeds the RP2040, flash and all LVC parts; +5V feeds the
  HCT parts and the amplifier.
- 33 Ω series resistors on the nine bus drivers (U6 outputs, U7 output).

## Checks the generator performs

Every pin of every part must be on exactly one net or in the NC list; every
net must have at least two members. It refuses to write files otherwise.
